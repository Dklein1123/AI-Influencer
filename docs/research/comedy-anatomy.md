# Sierra Comedy Anatomy — structural breakdowns of the lane

> The structural recipes from 8 working comedians in (or adjacent to)
> Sierra's lane. Every Sierra bit should pick a recipe, fill it with a
> trend, and ship. This doc replaces "make it funny" with "use template
> T6, here's the form."

> Source: research-agent deep-dive on Catherine Cohen / Esther Povitsky
> / Mary Beth Barone / Sarah Sherman / Jiaoying Summers / Hannah Berner /
> Tay Conti / Paige McKenzie. May 2026.

---

## 1. Why this doc exists

Earlier Sierra drafts (the "decenter men" v0 / v1 / v2) failed because
they tried to invent comedy from scratch. The fix isn't more inspiration
— it's STEALING THE STRUCTURE from comedians who already cracked the
form. None of these 8 own the conservative-women-comedy lane Sierra is
moving into, but each owns a structural recipe Sierra can lift.

**Use this doc:**
- Pick a trend
- Pick a template (T1–T7 below)
- Fill the template
- Ship

The bit isn't a joke. The bit is **a character doing something specific
that the audience recognizes themselves in.** Stop writing punchlines.
Start writing characters.

## 2. Eight comedians, eight structural recipes

### Catherine Cohen — Cabaret-Comedy

- **Structure:** Brag → confession → bigger brag. Sung-confessional-
  reversal. Open with hyper-confidence, pivot mid-line to the wound that
  produced it ("boys never wanted to kiss me / so now I do comedy"), then
  re-assert confidence louder.
- **Character:** Self-aware narcissist who knows she's coping. The pose
  IS the punchline.
- **Specificity:** Foot-pop "j'adore" gestures, named insecurities
  ("sobbing in a bikini"), branded girl-objects.
- **Visual/verbal:** Glamour pose UNDERCUT by panicked eyes. Visual stays
  glam; the lyric breaks.
- **Rule broken:** "Comedy = self-deprecation." Cohen self-aggrandizes
  louder than men do, then weaponizes the discomfort.
- **Sierra steal:** Sing the trend phrase as a confident lifestyle creed,
  drop the wound underneath in one line, pull the pose back up.

### Esther Povitsky — "Little Esther"

- **Structure:** Soft-voice + aggressive content. Setup in baby-pitch,
  weaponize via tone collapse — one word in lower register = the swerve.
- **Character:** A 5-foot "princess" who is secretly mean. Vulnerable
  surface, sharp ops underneath.
- **Specificity:** Self-naming ("Little Esther"), height mentions, named
  adversaries (siblings, exes).
- **Visual/verbal:** Doll-coded visual + adult-grievance content.
- **Rule broken:** "Cute = harmless." Cute is the delivery system for the
  kill.
- **Sierra steal:** Set up in a "good little girl" register, drop ONE
  adult/mean word that detonates the bit.

### Mary Beth Barone — "Drag His Ass"

- **Structure:** Diagnostic format — name a male archetype as a *case
  file*, prosecute. "Men who [specific behavior]." Punchline = the
  diagnosis is also self-indictment ("…and I keep dating them").
- **Character:** Deadpan prosecutor. Bored. Has seen this man before.
- **Specificity:** "Men in finance," "non-committal gaslighters," "serial
  DMers." The specificity IS the bit.
- **Visual/verbal:** Stillness + roast. Camera holds. No mug.
- **Rule broken:** "Roasts need energy." Hers are flat as a court
  transcript — that's the swerve.
- **Sierra steal:** "Men who [hyper-specific tic]" + "and I have dated 4
  of them this year alone."

### Sarah Sherman — SNL Update

- **Structure:** Frame-breaks via committed character. Enters Update in
  absurd costume/role, plays it 100% sincere, lets the institution be
  straight man. The bit is the GAP between commitment and setting.
- **Character:** Total commitment to a deranged premise. Never winks.
- **Specificity:** "Underwear in packs of 12 at CVS," "the kind that
  looks like a flag after the war." Domestic + grotesque.
- **Visual/verbal:** Visual (costume) commits HARDER than verbal. Verbal
  stays even.
- **Rule broken:** "Match your tone to your visual." She refuses.
- **Sierra steal:** Show up in full character costume for a "serious"
  take. Visual does 60% of the work; deadpan the script.

### Jiaoying Summers — Conservative-Lane Stand-Up

- **Structure:** Taboo-but-cute. Bright performative smile + the
  unsayable in the same tone. Punchlines are the *content*; the structure
  is the *delivery cushion*. Borrowed character voices (mom) launder a
  harder line.
- **Character:** Hot-girl ambassador who keeps saying things you can't
  say.
- **Specificity:** Named tiers ("Asian Tier System"), mom-voice quotes.
- **Visual/verbal:** Pageant-coded visual + content that contradicts
  pageant rules.
- **Rule broken:** "Pretty girls are safe." She uses pretty-girl delivery
  as cover for forbidden takes.
- **Sierra steal:** Smile through it. Use a borrowed character voice
  (mom, friend, hypothetical "girl in my DMs") to say the spicier half.

### Hannah Berner — "The Boys" Framework

- **Structure:** Group-noun + reversal. "I'm a [trend] apologist." State
  the supposed flaw. Reframe as preference. Self-indict on button.
- **Character:** Hot-mess-but-knowing. Sex-and-the-City voice for Gen Z.
- **Specificity:** Husband's exact age ("48 to my 32"), Disney princes by
  name.
- **Visual/verbal:** Casual-girl pose, polished punchlines.
- **Rule broken:** "You can't admit the cringe thing." She admits it
  on-mic and reframes it as taste.
- **Sierra steal:** "I'm a [trend] apologist" + list red flags + end on
  "and that's actually exactly what I want." This is the franchise
  engine.

### Tay Conti — Character-Commit TikToks

- **Structure:** POV-character monologue. Multiple roles in 30s; quick
  cuts, costume signals (hat on/off), accent flips. Each character speaks
  in own logic. Button = costume-drop reveal or a fourth role nobody saw.
- **Character:** Variable. The skill is the TRANSITIONS.
- **Specificity:** Hair flip = signal. Hat = signal. Visual grammar
  consistent across persona changes.
- **Visual/verbal:** Visual cuts carry structure; lines short.
- **Rule broken:** "One creator = one persona."
- **Sierra steal:** Two-voice TikTok. Sierra plays Sierra AND the trend's
  "ideal" version. Cut every 3 seconds.

### Paige McKenzie — "The Guy Who"

- **Structure:** Archetype-portrait. Title card names the male type ("the
  guy who…"), Sierra-as-him acts out 4-5 micro-tells. Final tell is one
  *she* does ("…wait that's me"). Self-indictment button.
- **Character:** Plays the man she's mocking, not herself.
- **Specificity:** Brand names, app names, exact phrases ("yeah, no, for
  sure").
- **Visual/verbal:** Mimicry — slouch, vape, vocal fry.
- **Rule broken:** "Make fun of them, not yourself." The flip is the bit.
- **Sierra steal:** "The girl who decenters men" — play that girl. Final
  tell = checks if he texted.

## 3. The 7 stealable templates (Sierra's recipes)

Pick one. Fill it with a trend. Ship.

| # | Name | Recipe | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1** | Cohen Cabaret Reversal | BRAG → WOUND → BIGGER BRAG | Self-aware narrative posts |
| **T2** | Povitsky Tone-Collapse | Baby-pitch (8s) → ONE adult-register word → return baby-pitch tag | Soft framing for sharp content |
| **T3** | Barone Diagnostic | "[Trend] girls are women who [tic], [tic], [tic] — and reader, I am one of them." | Prosecution + self-indictment |
| **T4** | Sherman Committed Costume | Full character visual + deadpan voiceover | When you can do costume/wardrobe |
| **T5** | Summers Smile-Through-Taboo | Pageant smile + character-voice ventriloquism (mom, ex, friend) | Spicier takes the algorithm doesn't ban |
| **T6** | **Berner Apologist Reframe** | "I'm a [trend] apologist" + list red flags as features + self-indict button | **WEEKLY ENGINE — repeats across trends** |
| **T7** | McKenzie Archetype-Flip | "The girl who [trend]" + 4 tells + 5th tell is YOU | When trend has a clear character to play |

**T6 is the highest-leverage** because it's a recipe that scales weekly
without exhausting a premise. Run it on: clean girl / tradwife / soft
launch / mob wife / old money / decenter men / divine feminine / etc.

## 4. Five Sierra-on-decenter-men takes (one per template)

Drafted by structural-research agent. The TAKE A (Berner Apologist) is
the operator's pick — strongest engine.

### TAKE A — T6 Berner Apologist  ⭐ recommended

```
[0–3s]   "I'm a decenter-men apologist."
[3–7s]   "He's not on my vision board. He's not in my 5-year plan."
[7–11s]  "I haven't thought about him in HOURS."
[11–14s] (glances at phone, dead-eyed) "He hasn't texted back since 2:14."
[14–18s] "It's 2:51. I am SO decentered."
Tag: "decentering men is going great for me ⏰"
```

**Why it lands:** the apologist frame is repeatable, the time-stamp
specific (2:14 → 2:51) is screenshot-able, and the audience IS the
character — they laugh because they recognize themselves.

## 5. The shipping rule

Every funny-mode Sierra post follows this checklist:

1. ☑️ Pick a template (T1–T7). State which.
2. ☑️ Pick a trend or topic.
3. ☑️ Fill the template — character does something specific.
4. ☑️ One screenshot-able tag line.
5. ☑️ One specific receipt (number, brand, name, time).
6. ☑️ Lint vs voice-profile §11.
7. ☑️ Render via from_trend.

If you can't articulate which template you're using, **don't ship the
post**. That's the rule that prevents the AI-comedy failure mode.
