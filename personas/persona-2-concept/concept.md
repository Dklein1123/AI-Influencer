## 1. Sub-archetype — pick one

The fitness/wellness lane is broad. We need to pick a specific archetype so
the persona is sharp, not generic. Each has different audience, different
brands, different content rhythm.

### Option A — The Pilates Princess (clean-girl wellness)
- **Vibe:** glowy, feminine, low-impact, neutral aesthetic, matcha
- **Audience:** women 22–32, urban, mid-to-high income, brunch-and-class
- **Content:** Pilates form, GRWM for class, what-I-eat, healthy-girl
  morning routine, slow-living
- **Brands:** Alo, Vuori, athleisure premium, Erewhon, sleep/skincare
- **Pros:** highest commercial fit, biggest creator-economy market right
  now, generates beautiful visual content
- **Cons:** crowded niche; she has to feel *singular* visually to break
  through

### Option B — Hot Girl Who Lifts (strength + protein)
- **Vibe:** athletic, strong-not-skinny, gym girl energy, slightly more
  masculine-adjacent
- **Audience:** women 20–30, fitness-serious, plus tech-bro/lifter
  crossover (bigger total reach)
- **Content:** lifting form, PRs, what-I-eat-to-grow, supplement reviews,
  "girls who lift" community content
- **Brands:** Alani Nu, Bloom Nutrition, GymShark, Optimum Nutrition,
  Ryze, BlackOps
- **Pros:** insane brand-deal density (supplement market is on fire),
  slightly less crowded than Pilates, audience converts hard on protein
  and supps
- **Cons:** harder to nail visually with AI (muscular shots are still
  uneven on Higgsfield); content needs more "how-to" expertise

### Option C — The Wellness Biohacker (protocol girl)
- **Vibe:** ice baths, sauna, supplements, cycle-syncing, "I'm 28 and you
  should listen to me about cortisol"
- **Audience:** women 28–40, higher-income, Andrew Huberman crossover
- **Content:** morning protocols, what-I-do-for-hormones, supplement
  stacks, cold plunge, sauna, sleep
- **Brands:** AG1, LMNT, Levels, Oura, Eight Sleep, Seed, Ritual
- **Pros:** highest CPMs in wellness, audience has money, less crowded
  than Pilates
- **Cons:** content is more knowledge-driven (need defensible takes),
  audience is older/sophisticated (harder to bullshit)

### Option D — The Yoga / Soft Wellness (spiritual-adjacent)
- **Vibe:** mindful, slower, breathwork, "your nervous system" content
- **Audience:** women 25–40, holistic-leaning, sometimes overlaps with
  woo-coded
- **Content:** yoga flows, breathwork, journaling, "reset your nervous
  system"
- **Brands:** Headspace/Calm, Lululemon, retreat brands, supplements
- **Pros:** very on-trend in 2026, sustainable audience, low controversy
- **Cons:** voice has to walk a line away from "manifesting" / woo
  vocabulary or it gets tired fast

### My recommendation: **Option A (Pilates Princess)** primary, **Option B
(Hot Girl Who Lifts)** as the close second.

A is the highest-revenue lane right now and the visual generation is
strongest there (Higgsfield does Pilates studios beautifully). B is
slightly less crowded but visually riskier with current AI tools. C and D
are great lanes but better as personas #3 or #4 once we've validated the
operational system.

**Decision needed:** A, B, C, or D? (Or a custom blend — but pure-archetype
beats blended for clarity.)

Sierra is blonde, light eyes, Florida polish. Persona #2 needs to look
**clearly different** so they don't feel like "the same girl with a
different vibe." Options below are deliberately distinct from Sierra.

### Variant 1 — Cool Brunette
- Dark chocolate brown hair, long, sleek straight or loose waves
- Brown eyes
- Cool-toned olive/neutral skin, less tan than Sierra
- Aesthetic: NYC / SoCal clean-girl, minimalist makeup, gold + silver mixed
- Body: lean Pilates body
- Reads as: 23–27, polished, urban

### Variant 2 — Athletic Auburn
- Auburn / red-brown hair, often in a slick ponytail
- Green eyes
- Lightly freckled, athletic skin
- Aesthetic: Aspen / Colorado wellness, athletic glow, more outdoorsy
- Body: stronger, more muscular than Sierra
- Reads as: 24–28, athletic, vibrant

### Variant 3 — Asian-American Clean Girl
- Sleek black or dark brown hair, long
- Dark brown eyes
- Olive / warm-neutral skin
- Aesthetic: minimalist clean-girl, Korean/Japanese skincare-coded
- Body: slim Pilates body
- Reads as: 24–28, polished, "expensive-looking"

### Variant 4 — Latina Wellness Girl
- Dark brown wavy hair, mid-length
- Brown eyes
- Warm tan skin (different undertone than Sierra's beachy tan)
- Aesthetic: Miami wellness, gold jewelry, sun-coded
- Body: curvy-fit, hourglass like Sierra but different proportions
- Reads as: 24–28, warm, magnetic

**Note on Latina archetype + Miami:** would be near Sierra geographically.
For operational separation we'd want a different city anyway (LA, Austin,
NYC). Easy to fix in prompts.

### My recommendation: depends on archetype choice
