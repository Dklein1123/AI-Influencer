>

>
> [07.5–10.0] The full report drops Sunday.
>
> [10.0–12.0] Link in bio. 🌴

Pacing: deadpan academic delivery on the first half. Lines 1–3 are the comedy structure (set-up · beat · punchline) — pause on "On the dating market." for half a beat to let "market research" register as deliberately formal. "Findings: it's worse." lands flat. The CTA is matter-of-fact, not pitch-y.

This unit explicitly uses the **direct CTA** (subscribe link) per
voice-profile §6. That's the trade-off of Pillar 4 — direct ask,
soft framing. Don't apologize for the ask. Don't repeat the ask twice
in the caption — the spoken line + the on-screen text + the bio link
is enough.

The mock-academic device makes the CTA feel like the *next chapter*, not
a sales pitch. Use it.
