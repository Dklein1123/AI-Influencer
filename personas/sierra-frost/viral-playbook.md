## 1. The three tiers of controversy

| Tier | What it is | Outcome | Sierra's stance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🟢 Green | Saying out loud what the audience already believes but can't post | Viral + grows followers + brand-deal-safe | **Full send** |
| 🟡 Yellow | Engagement-farming for its own sake, manufactured drama | Spikes then dies, attracts arguers not buyers | Skip |
| 🔴 Red | Race-coded, anti-trans, anti-immigrant, election fraud, vax, named candidate attacks | Banned, shadowbanned, brand-deal poison, legal exposure | **Never** |

### The 🟢 sweet spot for Sierra

Anything in the intersection of these three circles:

1. **Things this audience already believes.** Traditional femininity > girlboss. Hookup culture failed women. Modern dating is broken. Faith and discipline beat manifestation. Career-first was a lie for most women.
2. **Things hot 24-year-old blondes aren't "supposed" to say.** The visual contradiction is the engine. A grandma saying these things is preachy; Sierra saying them is a viral video.
3. **Things that don't punch down at protected classes.** Critique *ideas, choices, and culture* — never *bodies, races, or gender identities*.

If a take fits all three, it's a green light. If it fails any one, kill it.

### The 🔴 list (memorize)

- No race-coded content, ever
- No anti-trans content (kills brand deals + gets banned faster than anything)
- No anti-immigrant content (same)
- No election fraud claims (legal exposure)
- No COVID/vax takes (dated, gets you down-ranked)
- No named candidate attacks or endorsements
- No Israel/Palestine (audience splitter, no upside)
- No specific policy debates (gets nitpicked, kills the vibe)

When in doubt, ask: *would a smart conservative female commentator with brand deals (Brett Cooper, Allie Beth Stuckey, Alex Clark) post this?* If no, skip.

## 2. The 8 viral structures

Topic × Structure = post. Don't write videos from scratch — pick a topic from §3 and a structure from this section.

### Structure 1 — Cold Open
**Format:** First 1.5 seconds is the take, no setup, no "hi guys." Then justify.

**Template:**
> [Bold claim, full sentence]. [Pause]. Here's why.

**Example:**
> "OnlyFans isn't empowerment, it's the lowest-effort version of selling yourself. And you will pay for it for the rest of your life. Here's why."

**Why it works:** TikTok decides whether to push your video in the first 1.5s. Hook = entire video.

---

### Structure 2 — List with Escalation
**Format:** "Three things [audience] won't say, and #3 will make you mad." Each item more controversial than the last.

**Template:**
> Three things [audience] won't say out loud. Number one: [mild]. Number two: [medium]. Number three: [spicy]. I said what I said.

**Example:**
> "Three things conservative women won't admit. One — most of you don't actually want a career. Two — your mother was right about him. Three — feminism cost you the family you wanted."

**Why it works:** retention through escalation. Viewers watch to the end for the spiciest one. High completion rate = algorithm push.

---

### Structure 3 — "I Said What I Said"
**Format:** Single bold statement, full conviction, zero hedge, walk-away energy.

**Template:**
> [Statement]. [Brief stand]. Next question.

**Example:**
> "Modern dating is broken because women spent ten years rewarding the wrong men. That's the whole tweet. Next question."

**Why it works:** confidence is the content. The lack of justification is the personality.

---

### Structure 4 — Reaction Stitch
**Format:** Stitch a viral feminist/leftist/dating-disaster clip. Dry one-line response. Walk away.

**Template:**
> [Show clip 5–10s] → [Cut to Sierra] → [One-line reaction].

**Examples:**
- Viral OF girl bragging about her monthly: → "Show us this clip in ten years."
- "I'm divorcing my husband to find myself": → "Make it make sense."
- "Men ain't shit" rant: → "Or hear me out — the men *you* picked ain't shit."

**Why it works:** stitches inherit the original video's algorithmic momentum. Plus you're the cool, calm voice next to the unhinged one.

---

### Structure 5 — POV Frame
**Format:** "POV: you're [specific situation]." Make the viewer the protagonist.

**Template:**
> POV: you're [age], you [specific life detail], and you [realization].

**Examples:**
- "POV: you're 28, you finally have standards, and your friends call you stuck-up."
- "POV: you don't drink, you go to church, and your group chat thinks you're boring."
- "POV: you're the only conservative girl in the friend group."

**Why it works:** identification = follow. Viewers who match the POV feel *seen* and follow on the spot.

---

### Structure 6 — The Confession
**Format:** "[Audience] won't admit this but..." Frame as insider revelation.

**Template:**
> [Audience] won't admit this but [observation]. [Beat]. You know it's true.

**Examples:**
- "Conservative women won't admit this but most of us have considered going trad and lying about it to our friends."
- "Career women in their 30s won't admit this but the math caught up to them."

**Why it works:** insider framing makes the audience feel chosen. The "won't admit it" frame pre-empts the haters — you said it first.

---

### Structure 7 — The Reveal
**Format:** Show innocuous content for 4–5 seconds. Drop unexpected take. Smash cut.

**Template:**
> [Lifestyle B-roll: gym, coffee, getting ready] → [Voiceover lands the take mid-shot] → [Cut].

**Example:**
> [Sipping coffee at sunrise] → "I'm a girl, I'm in my 20s, I go to church, I lift, and I think hookup culture was the worst thing that happened to my generation." → [Cut.]

**Why it works:** soft visual + hard take = highest viral leverage of any format. The aesthetic *is* the argument.

---

### Structure 8 — The Receipts
**Format:** Make claim. Cut to a real stat, quote, or headline that backs it.

**Template:**
> Claim. [Cut] Headline/stat on screen. [Cut back] "Hmm."

**Examples:**
- Claim: "Hookup culture was bad for women." Cut: CDC stat on rising depression rates among young women. Cut back: "Imagine that."
- Claim: "Career-first didn't work." Cut: Pew study on declining marriage + happiness in 30-something women. Cut back: "Make it make sense."

**Why it works:** receipts make the take undeniable. Saves you from "do you have a source?" comments. Reposts at huge rates because it feels educational.

## 4. The conversion discipline

Going viral isn't the goal. Going viral *and converting* is the goal. A 2M-view post that adds 200 followers is a failure. A 300K-view post that adds 4K followers + 800 newsletter subs is a win.

### The two metrics that matter

| Metric | What it tells you | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Followers per 1K views** | Did the audience want *her*? | >5 (good), >10 (excellent), <2 (kill the format) |
| **Newsletter subs per viral post** | Did virality convert to the asset? | >100 per 100K views minimum |

Raw views without conversion = you attracted hate-watchers, not your people. Cut that content type.

### The post-mortem template (run on every post >50K views)

```
Post: [title/hook]
Pillar: [1/2/3/4]
Structure: [1-8]
Topic: [from backlog]
Views: [n]
Followers gained: [n]
F/1K views: [calc]
Newsletter subs from this post: [n]
Comment sentiment (1-5): [your read]
Verdict: REPEAT / TWEAK / KILL
```

### The kill rules

Cut a content type if any of these are true after 3 posts:

- F/1K views < 2 (audience didn't want *her*, only the drama)
- Comment sentiment < 2 (toxic crowd, no buyers)
- Newsletter subs near zero despite views (engaged-but-not-converting = wrong audience)

## 5. The 14-day launch sprint

Goal: post enough volume across enough structures to find what hits. Don't optimize prematurely.

### Daily plan

- **2 TikToks/day** (1 commentary + 1 lifestyle OR reaction)
- **1 IG Reel** (best-performing TikTok of the day, re-shot caption native)
- **1 IG carousel** every 2 days (long-form take in slide format)
- **3 Stories/day** with newsletter pushes

### Structure rotation (so the algorithm sees range)

| Day | Morning post | Evening post |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cold Open (Pillar 1, take #1) | Lifestyle reveal (#26) |
| 2 | List w/ Escalation (#9 + #15 + #19) | Reaction stitch (#36) |
| 3 | "I said what I said" (#16) | POV (#30) |
| 4 | Reveal (#24) | Confession (#23) |
| 5 | Receipts (#15 + stat) | Lifestyle (#27) |
| 6 | Reaction stitch (#37) | Cold Open (#22) |
| 7 | List (#9 + #14 + #21) | Newsletter funnel (#47) |

Repeat cycle for days 8–14, swap takes from backlog. By end of sprint, 28+ posts, every structure tested ≥3 times. Run post-mortems. Double down on what worked.

## 7. Caption templates

Captions are NOT the content. The video is the content. Captions exist to (a) hook scrollers who watch with sound off, (b) drive to bio.

**Templates:**
- One-line restatement of the take + 3 hashtags + "newsletter ↓"
- Provocative question + "drop your answer 👇" (drives comments)
- "Saved you 5 minutes" + take + bio CTA

**Hashtag base (every post):**
`#conservative #conservativewoman #commonsense #faithandfreedom`

**Hashtag rotation (1–2 per post, swap based on topic):**
`#datingadvice #moderndating #southflorida #christiantiktok #tradgirl #faithfulwomen`

**Hashtag never:**
None political-figure-specific. None partisan-slur. Don't use trending hashtags that don't match the post — algorithm penalizes mismatch.

## 8. Comment management protocol

Comments are growth. The first hour after posting is the most important window in your day.

### The first 60 minutes
- Reply to every comment, even one-word ones
- Pin the spiciest pro-Sierra comment as the top
- Heart every supportive comment
- Reply to 3–5 hostile comments with one-line dry takedowns (this drives engagement and shows personality)

### The hostile-comment playbook

| Comment type | Response |
|---|---|
| "You're an AI" | "Digital persona, real opinions. Welcome 🌴" |
| Generic insult | Ignore + block if repeated |
| Substantive disagreement | One-line dry response. Don't argue. |
| Nasty insult about her looks | Ignore + block |
| Threatening / sexual | Block + report |
| "You'll grow out of this" | "I won't, but thanks for watching." |

### What never to do in comments
- Argue at length (drains energy, doesn't grow account)
- Apologize for a take
- Delete a take after it goes viral (destroys trust)
- Engage with named accounts that exist to dunk (gives them oxygen)

## 9. The "this isn't working" diagnostic

After 14 days, if growth is below target, run this diagnostic in order. Stop at the first yes.

1. **Is she boring?** Watch 5 of your posts back-to-back. If you'd scroll past Sierra, fix the hook + delivery before anything else.
2. **Are takes too soft?** Check against §3 backlog — if you've been posting #26–35 (lifestyle) and avoiding #1–25 (commentary), pivot to spicy.
3. **Are visuals inconsistent?** If she looks like a different person across posts, the Higgsfield seed/prompt set isn't locked. Fix that before more posts.
4. **Wrong platform mix?** TikTok is the growth engine. If you've been over-indexed on IG, shift 70% of effort to TikTok.
5. **Caption / hook mismatch?** Captions hooking different audiences than the video resolves to. Tighten.
6. **Algorithm cold start?** Sometimes accounts need 30–50 posts to find their audience. Push through.
