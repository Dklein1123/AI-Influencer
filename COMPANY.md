# Company Spec — Multi-Persona AI Media Operation

> The strategic bible for the company that runs Sierra Frost and the eventual
> portfolio of personas. This doc lives at the repo root because it's the
> only doc that crosses persona boundaries. Persona-specific specs live in
> `personas/[name]/`.

**Status:** v0.1 — Sierra Frost is persona #1 and the test case. The system
defined here is what scales after Sierra proves the model.

---

## 1. The thesis

**There is a real, repeatable arbitrage in operating AI personas as a
portfolio business.** Three legs:

1. **AI generation has crossed the consistency threshold.** Higgsfield Soul
   ID + Hedra lip-sync + ElevenLabs voice locks mean a single operator can
   maintain visually consistent personas indefinitely — something that
   required a person + a photographer + a manager 24 months ago.
2. **Niche audiences pay 1.5–3x mainstream rates and are systematically
   underserved by AI personas.** Conservative lifestyle, faith-forward
   wellness, modest fashion, finance-curious women — all have buying
   audiences and zero AI-persona supply.
3. **The system itself is the moat, not any one persona.** Single AI
   personas are volatile single assets. A portfolio + a launch playbook +
   accumulating learnings = a defensible operation.

The company isn't an "AI girl with an OnlyFans." It's a media operation
that happens to use AI to lower production costs to near-zero, executing the
same persona-portfolio strategy that talent agencies, MCN networks, and
old-school media companies have always run — just with infrastructure none
of them have.

---

## 2. Company positioning

### What the company is
A **disclosed-AI media operation** running 5–10 niche-specific personas
across underserved demographics, monetized primarily via affiliate, brand
partnerships, newsletters, and digital products.

### What the company is NOT
- An "OnlyFans agency" — Lane B / adult-tier personas are deliberately
  separated and not the focus.
- A SaaS for influencers — the moat is operating, not selling tools.
- A service business — no client work. We own every persona we run.

### The visible vs. invisible question

**The company itself stays invisible for now.** No public brand. No "behind
Sierra Frost is Acme Media Co" coming-out-day for the next 18 months. Each
persona operates as if alone. This is for three reasons:

1. **Operational isolation.** If one persona blows up (banned, exposed,
   scandalized), it cannot contaminate the others.
2. **Audience trust.** Conservative-lane audiences in particular don't want
   to know there's a marketing operation behind their favorite creator.
3. **Optionality.** We can come out as a company on our own timeline (e.g.,
   when raising capital or pursuing acquisition) — earlier is irreversible.

The company exists as a legal entity (see §10) but does not publish, post,
or get tagged. Personas are the public surface.

## 3. Portfolio strategy

### Lane diversification principle

Each persona occupies a distinct **lane × audience × monetization** triplet.
Diversification protects against:
- **Platform risk** (a TikTok algorithm change hits all conservative
  creators)
- **Niche fatigue** (audiences cycle every 12–24 months)
- **Regulatory risk** (one niche gets new disclosure laws)
- **Ad-market risk** (a category's brand spend dries up)

### Recommended portfolio (in suggested launch order)

| # | Working name | Lane | Audience | Monetization | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sierra Frost** | Conservative lifestyle commentator | Conservative women 22–45, US | Affiliate (conservative-niche), brand deals, newsletter, digital products | High (audience volatility) |
| 2 | TBD — fitness/wellness | Pilates / clean-girl wellness | Women 22–35, mainstream | Supplements, athleisure, wellness apps | Low — biggest market, slowest controversy |
| 3 | TBD — cottagecore homemaker | Recipes, home, faith-coded but apolitical | Women 25–45, married/mom demographic | Kitchen brands, home goods, faith adjacent | Low |
| 4 | TBD — finance/crypto girl | "Money-curious girlie" | Women 25–40 + tech-bro crossover | Fintech, crypto products (insane CPMs), courses | Medium |
| 5 | TBD — travel/luxury girl | Resort + luxury travel | Aspirational women 25–45 | Hotel comps, tourism boards, luxury fashion | Low |

**Why this order:**
- Sierra first because she's already started + the lane is empty (first-
  mover value)
- Fitness/wellness second because it's the *easiest* to monetize and grow,
  fastest revenue add
- Cottagecore third because it shares prompt infrastructure with Sierra
  (same aesthetic toolkit, different voice + demographic)
- Finance/crypto fourth because CPMs are unreal but execution is
  hardest (need credible-feeling commentary, not just visuals)
- Travel last because it requires the most varied generation (locations) and
  is best built on top of operational maturity

### What's NOT in the portfolio
- Adult / OnlyFans-style personas — different operation, different legal
  surface, kept separate or skipped entirely
- Political commentary beyond Sierra's lane — too much regulatory risk
- News / journalism personas — explicitly off-limits given current AI
  impersonation laws
- Personas tied to specific real people (no AI Kim Kardashian etc.)

### Portfolio target — 24 months out
- 5 active personas
- 1 in active sunset (graceful wind-down replaced by new launch)
- ~$50–150K/mo combined revenue
- Operated by 1 founder + 2 VAs + 1 part-time editor

## 4. The system as moat

What makes this defensible vs. someone copying tomorrow:

### Layer 1 — The persona library (the asset)
Trained Soul IDs, locked voices, locked seeds, refined prompts per persona.
Each represents months of generation iteration. **Not transferable, not
reverse-engineerable from public posts alone.**

### Layer 2 — The accumulated playbooks
The viral-playbook hooks/structures that work get refined post-mortem-by-
post-mortem. After 1,000 posts, we know with statistical confidence which
hooks convert at what rate. This is proprietary data nobody else has.

### Layer 3 — The brand relationship graph
Every brand we work with on persona #1 is a warm intro for personas #2–5.
By persona #3, we're not cold-pitching anymore. The brand contact list is a
proper asset.

### Layer 4 — The newsletter subscriber base
Cross-promote (carefully, segmented by demographic overlap) — Sierra's
audience that ALSO loves the homemaker persona is a real cross-sell.

### Layer 5 — The launch playbook
The 30-day persona launch (§6) is the IP. As we run it for personas #2,
#3, #4, the playbook tightens. By persona #5, launch-to-revenue is days
not months.

### Layer 6 — Tooling
Custom posting/scheduling/analytics built for our portfolio specifically.
Not sellable as SaaS, but a 10x productivity multiplier for the operator.

**None of these layers are individually defensible — together they're the
moat.**

## 5. Per-persona lifecycle

A persona is not a forever asset. They have a lifecycle and we manage it
deliberately.

| Phase | Duration | Goal | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Concept** | 1–2 weeks | Persona bible drafted, look generated, voice locked | Fully on paper before any post |
| **Soft launch** | 30 days | First 30 posts, find what hits, no monetization push | Identity-lock, audience-discovery |
| **Ramp** | Months 2–6 | Aggressive growth, monetization layered in | The hard work phase |
| **Scale** | Months 6–18 | Brand deals at scale, newsletter monetizes hard | Highest revenue per hour |
| **Mature** | Months 18–36 | Stable revenue, refresh content, anti-fatigue | Persona refresh tactics |
| **Sunset** | Months 30+ | Audience starts to fade, we pivot or wind down | Don't fight gravity |
| **Sunset graceful** | 1–3 months | Newsletter migrated to next persona, archives kept | Capture residual value |

**Sunset is not failure** — it's recognizing that audience attention is
finite per persona. Better to launch a fresh one than ride a declining one
into the ground.

## 6. The 30-day persona launch playbook

This is the IP. Codify, refine, repeat.

### Week -2 to 0 (concept phase)
- [ ] Pick lane × audience × monetization (deliberately uncrowded by other
      AI personas)
- [ ] Name + handle — check availability across TikTok, IG, Threads, X,
      newsletter platforms
- [ ] Write persona bible (use Sierra's as the template — it's the model)
- [ ] Generate 20 reference images, train Soul ID v1
- [ ] Lock voice in ElevenLabs
- [ ] Lock visual prompt library v0.1 (50 templates)
- [ ] Lock viral playbook v0.1 (50-take backlog, 8 structures, hook library)
- [ ] Set up: TikTok, IG, beehiiv newsletter, Stan.store, all linked
- [ ] Bio copy with disclosure ready

### Day 1–14 (soft launch)
- [ ] Post 2x/day on TikTok, 1x/day IG Reel + 3x/week carousel
- [ ] Run the 14-day sprint matrix from `viral-playbook.md`
- [ ] Reply to every comment in first 60 minutes after post
- [ ] No monetization push yet — earning trust + algorithmic warm-up

### Day 15–30 (validation + monetization on)
- [ ] Apply to 5 affiliate programs in the niche
- [ ] Linktr.ee replaces with Stan.store, affiliate links seeded
- [ ] Newsletter launches — Sunday 1 = welcome + persona-defining take
- [ ] First digital product live ($15–25 PDF)
- [ ] First brand DM outreach to 10 small brands in niche
- [ ] Run post-mortems on top 5 + bottom 5 posts; update playbook v0.2

### Day 30 — gate decision
- **Green:** combined platforms >5K followers, NL >300, ≥1 affiliate sale,
  >2% F/1K-views ratio. → Continue, ramp aggressively.
- **Yellow:** growth meaningful but slow. → Tweak hooks/visuals, run
  another 30 days.
- **Red:** no follower growth, no engagement. → Diagnose (use the "this
  isn't working" diagnostic). If audience just doesn't exist, kill the
  persona at day 60 max.

## 7. Operational separation between personas

Hard rules to keep personas isolated:

| Element | Per persona |
|---|---|
| Email address | Yes — `sierra@[domain]`, `[name2]@[domain]` |
| Bank / payment account | Single company account, but separate Stripe / payout records per persona |
| Phone number | Per persona where possible (Google Voice fine) |
| Stan.store / linktr.ee | Per persona |
| Brand contacts / CRM | Per persona, with a "shared" warm-intro layer |
| Posting devices / accounts | Logically separated — no cross-tagging, no follow each other |
| Newsletter | Per persona |
| Voice + visual identity | Per persona, never reused |

**Cross-promotion is forbidden until each persona has independent traction.**
Don't have Sierra promote persona #2's account at launch. The connection
becomes the story if anything goes wrong. We can revisit at scale.

## 8. Tech stack — buy vs. build

### Buy (use existing tools — don't reinvent)
| Function | Tool | Cost/mo |
|---|---|---|
| Image/video generation | Higgsfield | ~$50 |
| Talking-head lip sync | Hedra Character-3 | ~$30 |
| Voice synthesis | ElevenLabs | ~$30 |
| Newsletter | beehiiv | $0–50 |
| Linktree replacement | Stan.store | ~$30 |
| Posting scheduler | Metricool or Buffer | ~$30 |
| Print-on-demand | Printful | $0 + COGS |
| Digital product hosting | Stan.store / Gumroad | included |
| Email (per-persona) | Google Workspace | ~$6/persona |
| Analytics | Native platform + Metricool | included |
| Domain + hosting | Cloudflare + Vercel | ~$20 |

**Approx. tooling cost: $250–350/mo per persona + shared.** Trivial vs.
revenue at scale.

### Build (custom — these become the moat)
| Function | Why build | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| **Generation pipeline** — wraps Higgsfield API, applies persona base block + negative prompt + style suffix automatically | Saves 80% of generation-session time | Phase 2 |
| **Idea/trend engine** — scrapes TikTok trends, conservative news cycle, dating discourse, suggests on-pillar takes | The hardest part of daily content is "what to post"; automating it is the highest-leverage build | Phase 2 |
| **Post analytics aggregator** — pulls TikTok + IG + newsletter metrics into a single dashboard, runs auto post-mortems | Cuts post-mortem time, makes patterns visible | Phase 3 |
| **Cross-persona orchestrator** — runs the daily/weekly cadence checklist for every persona, surfaces what needs attention | Required at 3+ personas | Phase 3 |
| **Brand CRM with persona-tagged outreach** | Required at 5+ brand campaigns active | Phase 4 |

### Build phases (sequential)

**Phase 0 (now):** Sierra running fully manually. Write down everything
that works. No code yet.

**Phase 1 (Sierra month 2–3):** Validate the persona model. Capture metrics
manually. Identify what's repetitive.

**Phase 2 (Sierra month 4 + persona #2 concept):** Build generation
pipeline + idea/trend engine. These are the tools that make the 30-day
launch playbook actually fast.

**Phase 3 (3 personas live):** Analytics aggregator + cross-persona
orchestrator. Operations become a dashboard, not a notebook.

**Phase 4 (5 personas live):** Brand CRM + automation of the brand
outreach pipeline. By here we're a real operation.

**Don't build before each phase's prerequisite is met.** Premature tooling
is the #1 killer of small media operations.

## 10. Legal & financial structure

### Recommended structure (talk to a real lawyer — this is direction, not
advice)

- **Single LLC** (Wyoming or Delaware) holds all personas under it. Cleanest
  for small-scale start. Reconsider per-persona LLC at $50K+/mo per
  persona for liability isolation.
- **EIN + business bank account.** Personas all bill to the same entity.
- **Operating agreement** including AI-disclosure as a baked-in operational
  rule.
- **Trademarks** on persona names + handles once they cross meaningful
  follower thresholds (~50K).

### Insurance
- **Media liability / E&O insurance** at $50K+/mo revenue. Covers right-of-
  publicity, defamation, IP claims. ~$1–3K/year.

### Contracts (templates needed in `shared/legal/`)
- Brand partnership template (Sierra version exists in pitch templates)
- Talent / VA agreement (when hiring)
- AI generation tool terms-of-service summary (so we don't violate any
  upstream)

### Tax
- Schedule C or LLC pass-through at low scale. S-Corp election when
  margin > $100K profit (saves on self-employment tax). CPA conversation
  by month 6.

## 13. Eventual exits (for context — not pressing)

What this can become:
1. **Cash-flowing operation.** Run it indefinitely at $500K–2M/yr profit
   with 1 operator + 2 VAs. Most likely default outcome.
2. **Acqui-hire / strategic acquisition.** Media companies, MCN networks,
   conservative-media holdcos may pay 3–5x annual revenue for a portfolio
   with healthy audiences and a documented operational system. ~$2M–10M
   range at scale.
3. **License the system.** Sell the persona-launch playbook + tooling to
   other operators. Lower-margin than running it, higher leverage if you
   want to step out.
4. **Build a SaaS on top.** The tooling we build in Phase 2–4 has real
   value to other AI-persona operators. Spin out as a separate company.

None of these are decisions for now. **Optionality is the goal.** Build the
operation in a way that any of them remain available.
